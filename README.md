# EXCEL

## Bike Sales and Customer Purchasing Behavior
Business Task

Clean raw customer data and utilize Excel data cleaning and pivot tables to analyse demographics, income, and commuting trends—connecting foundational data analytics principles to identify the key drivers behind bike purchases.

## Dashboard Overview & Key Metrics

Age Bracket Analysis: Tracks purchasing volume across different age groups (adolescent, middle age, and old).

Average Income per Purchase: Compares average customer income by gender for both bike buyers and non-buyers.

Customer Commute: Evaluates the relationship between daily commute distance and purchase likelihood.

Interactive Slicers: Enables dynamic filtering by Marital Status, Education Level, and Region.
